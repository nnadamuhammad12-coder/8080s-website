Form.

A section through me.

01 — 05 / ongoing

The things that shape my work begin outside it. Movement, people, small details and a willingness to try something unexpected. This is a look at the person behind the projects.

AA
01 / MOVEMENT

Finding my balance.

A.01

Golf and skiing show the athletic, playful side of me. I like the mix of concentration and letting go: finding a rhythm, trying again, enjoying being outside.

02 / OPEN WATER

Room to breathe.

A.02

Boats, water and good company. This is the adventurous, social side of me: making room for fun, a change of scenery and the people I share it with.

03 / DETAIL

It’s in the connections.

A.03
DETAIL 01 / NOT TO SCALE

My technical style lives in the details: the meeting of metal, leather and fabric; the mechanism of a watch; the character of an object. I’m drawn to how something is made as much as how it looks.

04 / FOUNDATIONS

What holds it all together.

A.04
THE BEARING STRUCTURE

Family is my foundation. This photograph with my grandparents represents the love and values I carry with me. The people around the table matter as much as anything I make.

a constant, wherever I am.
05 / EXPERIMENTATION

Leave a little room for strange.

A.05

The experimental, slightly crazy side of me. Trying things on, playing with identity and enjoying the unexpected. Not everything needs to begin with a serious explanation.
